Setting up your books

Your currency, your minimum balance, your accounts, income and bills, step by step.

On this page
  • When to use it
  • Step by step
  • Good to know
  • Related

Set-up tells Damla what it needs to give a useful answer: which currency your books are in, what you keep as a minimum, and what you own, earn and owe. Do it in one sitting or in pieces. Anything you skip can be done later.

Whatever you leave for later stays a to-do. Today shows a Finish setting up row until it is done.

When to use it

  • The first time you open Damla, to get to your first forecast.
  • When Today shows Finish setting up, to fill in something you skipped.
  • When something changed in your life - a new card, a loan, a bill that repeats - and you want to add it in the guided way.
  • You would rather not fill forms: every step except Investments and assets also takes a plain sentence.

Step by step

  1. Open Today. If it is empty, tap Set up step by step. Later you can reopen the same interview from Settings under Set-up guide.
  2. On the first screen (Welcome to Damla) choose Your main currency - the currency of your region is suggested when Damla knows it.
  3. Type the Minimum balance to keep - the amount you want untouched. Everything above it is what the app calls safe to spend. You can change the minimum later.
  4. Tap Start, or instead open a family snapshot (a read-only picture another user of Damla sent you), or choose Look around with invented numbers until you are ready to use your own figures.
  5. The next screen asks What shall we set up?, with six things you can tick: Bank accounts and cash, Regular income, Credit cards, Regular spending, Loans and debts, Investments and assets. Tick what applies to you and tap Start. You can also choose Add statements instead to import PDF or CSV statements.
  6. Do one screen per ticked part. Each has its own add button - Add an account, Add income, Add a card, Add a bill, Add (a menu: a loan with instalments, or money with a person), Add a holding - plus Next at the bottom (or I don't have any if that part turned out to be empty) and Do this later.
  7. On every screen except Investments and assets you can instead type a sentence in the Or just type it box and tap Read it. Damla reads it on the phone, fills the form and shows what it understood for you to check before anything is saved.
  8. At the end you reach All set for now: your first forecast, what it rests on, and anything you postponed as buttons you can go back to. Set up automatic capture opens the step-by-step wizard for bank texts, emails and Wallet payments; you can also do that later from Settings. Tap Go to Today when you are done.

What each part asks for, so you can prepare:

  • Bank accounts and cash: each bank account, savings account and cash you hold, with its balance today.
  • Regular income: salary, rent you receive, anything that arrives on a schedule, with its amount and next date.
  • Credit cards: what you owe now (split into what is already billed and what is not yet), the Statement date and the Payment due date (each a day of the month), and which account pays the card.
  • Regular spending: rent, school, utilities, phone, insurance, subscriptions: what leaves on a schedule.
  • Loans and debts: a loan with instalments - what is still to repay, the instalment and how many are left - or money that stands between you and a person, either you owe them or they owe you.
  • Investments and assets: shares, gold, property, a car - anything you own that has a value. You keep the values up to date yourself.

Tip: When a step needs an account to move the money through and you have not added one yet, the app says so and the add button waits until you do. Nothing is lost by doing accounts first.

Good to know

Note: You can leave and come back at any point. Progress is saved with the book. Reopening from Today picks up at the first step you postponed; the Set-up guide row in Settings starts again from the step list.

Tip: Adding statements is optional in set-up. Once your accounts exist you can import them any time to fill in history and check your numbers.

Note: The gaps the first forecast lists (for example no account yet, or no income set) are not errors. Add the missing piece and the gap goes away.

Your minimum balance lives in Settings under Basics. Change it there whenever the number you want to keep changes.
