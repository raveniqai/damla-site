What Damla is for

The idea in one page, and a map of the app.

On this page
  • When to use it
  • The five tabs
  • Good to know
  • Related

Damla is a cash-flow app that lives on your iPhone. It answers one question every day: will you have enough money until your next income, and what happens if you spend, commit to something or delay something.

You tell Damla what happens with your money - a sentence, a form, a bank alert or a pasted message. Damla keeps the books correctly: every amount moves between two places, so nothing is counted twice. All the maths - balances, instalments, forecasts, gains - is done by ordinary, tested code. The on-device model only helps read your words. Your data stays on the phone: there is no account, no server and no tracking.

When to use it

  • Every day, to see the one number that matters: how much is safe to spend.
  • Before you buy something or commit to a payment, to see what it does to the weeks ahead.
  • When a bank message arrives, so the transaction is recorded without typing it in.
  • When you want to know where the money went, what you own, or whether you can afford something you have in mind.

The five tabs

The app has five tabs at the bottom.

  1. Open Damla. It asks for Face ID when you open it, and again only after it has been away for the length of time you choose in Settings under Basics, Ask for Face ID (every time, or after 1, 5 or 15 minutes). The Shortcut automations that pass bank messages to Damla do not need it.
  2. Today is where you land. The big teal card shows Safe to spend, whether your balance stays above your minimum, and a small chart of your balance ahead (tap it for the full Forecast and What if). Below it you find what is coming, and anything waiting for you.
  3. Tap Activity to see every transaction. Tap one to change it.
  4. Tap Accounts to see your banks, savings, cards, loans, investments, assets and people.
  5. Tap Insights to see where the money went, your budget, net worth and holdings.
  6. Tap Plan to see everything that commits future money: income and bills, instalment plans, staged purchases, bills coming, and people you lent to or owe.
  7. On Today, the gear icon at the top right opens Settings, where you also find the guide, your backup and the set-up guide. The book icon next to it opens the guide directly.

Good to know

Note: Anything that creates a commitment (an instalment plan, a loan), or that Damla is unsure about, is shown for one tap before it is written.

Tip: When you have no accounts yet, Today shows a Let's set up Damla screen with a Set up step by step button. The set-up guide can be reopened later from Today or from Settings.

Note: The app gives you information and estimates about your own spending. It does not give financial advice; what to spend is your decision.

No account, balance, transaction or note ever leaves the phone, and the app works fully offline. The only lookups are the few optional ones, and they send no personal data - see Privacy and your data.
