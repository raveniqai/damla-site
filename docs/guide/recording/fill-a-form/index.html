Fill a form

Record an expense, income or transfer with the form.

On this page
  • When to use it
  • Step by step
  • Changing something you recorded
  • Good to know
  • Related

The form records one thing that happened: money spent, money received, a move between your own accounts, or a card bill paid from a bank account. Use it when you want to check every field yourself, or to finish something Damla read but could not complete on its own.

When to use it

  • You want to record something by hand, field by field.
  • Damla read a sentence or a message but something was missing: Complete it opens this form with what was understood already filled in.
  • An item from the Inbox needs completing.
  • You want to change or correct something you recorded earlier.

Step by step

  1. Tap the plus button on Today or Activity.
  2. Tap Fill a form. The New entry screen opens.
  3. At the top, pick the kind. The four kinds are Spent, Received, Moved and Paid card.
  4. Type the amount and set the date.
  5. Choose the accounts, and a category for spending or income:

- Spent: choose Paid from - a bank, savings, wallet or card account - and a category. - Received: choose a category and the account the money goes Into. - Moved: choose From and To. The list holds your bank, savings and wallet accounts and people. They cannot be the same account. - Paid card: choose Paid from - a bank, savings or wallet account - and the Card.

  1. For Spent or Received you can add who you paid or who paid you ("Paid to (optional)" or "From whom (optional)") and a note.
  2. Tap Save.

Note: Moving money or paying a card is never counted as spending. A move only changes where the money sits; paying a card pays its bill.

If it was in another currency

For Spent or Received, once you have chosen the account, the form shows a Paid in another currency section. If the merchant charged a currency other than the account's, pick it under Charged in and type the Amount charged. Damla then offers Use the estimate: … - the amount worked out from the latest exchange rate and the bank's markup (set in Settings). A figure you type yourself replaces the estimate. If there is no exchange rate for that currency yet, type what the card was billed: it is on the statement or in the bank app.

For Moved between two accounts in different currencies, a Received section appears: type the Amount received in the receiving account's currency.

Changing something you recorded

  1. Open Activity and tap the transaction. The same form opens, titled Edit entry. (A transaction that is already split opens the split sheet instead.)
  2. Change what you need and tap Save. To remove it, swipe the transaction in the Activity list to delete it.

After you save a transaction with a category, Damla remembers that the shop or person belongs there, so the next alert from them is filed in that category on its own. If earlier transactions from the same shop are in a different category, Damla asks whether to change them too: Change them to … or Only this one. The fallback category Other is not remembered.

Good to know

  • Changing the kind after you have picked accounts clears them, so pick the kind first.
  • Under the Category picker, tap New category… to create one on the spot; it is selected right away.
  • The new-category sheet suggests an icon from the name - gym, pets, kids, insurance, repairs and many more have one built in - and lets you pick your own from a grid. The icon shows in Activity.
  • The same sheet opens from Settings, Categories, where you can also rename or remove categories later.
  • If you are completing a bank alert about a card or account Damla did not know yet, picking the account in the form teaches it those last digits: the next alert from it needs no question.
  • An amount that was only estimated from a rate is kept as an estimate; the statement replaces it with the amount the bank really billed. Type the real figure when you know it.

Tip: To share one payment among several categories (a supermarket shop that was groceries and school supplies), swipe it right in Activity and tap Split. To enter something you buy again and again, swipe it right and tap Duplicate: a new form opens with the same amount, account, category and payee, dated today, and nothing is saved until you tap Save. Moves between accounts, card payments and split transactions cannot be duplicated.
