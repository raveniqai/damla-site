Staged purchases

Off-plan property or anything paid in stages.

On this page
  • When to use it
  • Where to find it
  • Step by step
  • Good to know
  • Related

A staged purchase is Damla's record of something you pay for in dated stages, such as an off-plan apartment. It shows what you have paid and what is still to come. Payments still ahead go into your forecast, so the money is ready when each stage falls due.

When to use it

  • You are buying off-plan property and the developer asks for payments on known dates.
  • You are paying for anything else in stages, such as a plot of land or a big order paid in parts.
  • You have already paid something towards it, such as a down payment, and want it counted.
  • You want the property to become a holding in your net worth when it is handed over.

Where to find it

Open Plan, then Staged purchases. You can also reach a staged purchase from Accounts under Assets.

Step by step

Create one

  1. On the Staged purchases screen, tap Add (the plus button).
  2. Enter a name, the developer or seller (optional), the Total price, and the account you Usually paid from.
  3. Under Paid before Damla, enter what you already paid under Already paid and its Paid on date. It counts as paid, and no account balance changes.
  4. Under Payments still to come, add each dated payment you know about: a Due date and an Amount. Tap Add another payment for each further one.
  5. Tap Save.

The purchase's page shows a Paid so far card with a progress bar, the price, what is paid, what is Scheduled, and what is Not yet scheduled.

Keep it up to date

  1. Open the purchase.
  2. Tap a payment to change its date or amount, or swipe it to delete it. To add one, tap Add a payment.
  3. Tap the Price row to change the total price.
  4. If you forgot a payment you made before you started using Damla, use the Already paid before Damla section: enter the amount and the date, then tap Save.
  5. When you pay an instalment from an account, use Record a payment: enter the amount, choose Paid from, and tap Save. Recorded payments settle the oldest scheduled payments first.

Each payment in the list shows its date and amount and what is still outstanding: paid when nothing is left, or the outstanding amount in red with due now when it is overdue.

Hand over

  1. On the purchase's page, tap Handed over: move to Investments.
  2. Enter today's market value in the Value of the property field.
  3. Damla tells you how much of the scheduled payments will be removed from your forecast, or says nothing is left to pay.
  4. Tap Move to Investments to confirm.

The purchase stops being a staged purchase and becomes a holding: what you paid stays as its cost, and the market value you entered becomes its worth. See Property and cars.

Good to know

Note: Payments still to come are counted in your forecast on their dates, along with your bills and income. After handover those scheduled payments leave the forecast, and the property is counted at its value instead.

Tip: Enter only the payments you know about. You can add the later stages when the developer announces them. To change a date or an amount, tap the payment.

Limit: The figures on the page come from what you entered: the total price, the payments you scheduled and the payments you recorded. If a stage changes, tap the payment and correct it.

Note: Damla gives information and estimates from your own books. It does not give financial or investment advice.
