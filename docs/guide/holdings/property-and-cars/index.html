Property and cars

Track things that lose or gain value with your own figures.

On this page
  • When to use it
  • Step by step
  • Reading the value
  • Good to know
  • Related

Property, vehicles and other things you own — a gold ring, a plot of land — live in Damla as holdings, with a value you keep up to date yourself. Damla does not guess what they are worth: only shares and funds have a price it can look up. The figures shown are the ones you give, so your value is as current as your last update. Damla gives you information and estimates about your own books, not valuations or advice.

When to use it

  • You own a home, an apartment, land or a car and want it counted in your net worth.
  • You receive rent and want to see what it brought in and the yield against the property's value.
  • You are buying something big with a loan and want the down payment, the loan and the instalments set up together.
  • You want a record of how the value has changed since you last updated it.

Step by step

Add a property you own

  1. Open Insights, then Holdings, then tap the Add button (the plus sign).
  2. Choose the Type: Property. Name it, leave the quantity at 1, and enter the value of one unit today.
  3. In What it cost (optional), enter what you paid if you want to see the gain against it.
  4. Under Paid from, choose Already owned, so no account balance changes.

Holdings of other types, such as stocks or gold, are added the same way; see Investments and holdings.

Link the rent you receive

  1. Open Plan, then Income & bills, and tap the plus button. Add the rent as income: the amount, the account it is paid into, and how often it repeats (every month, every 3 months, or Custom… for a rhythm of your own).
  2. Open the property's page and go to its Rent section. Choose what the rent is booked to. If nothing is linked yet, tap Create “Rent from …” and link it, and Damla makes the income category and links it in one step.
  3. Record rent as income in that category, or let a bank alert or a statement add it — Damla adds up whatever is booked there.

The property's page then shows Rent, last 12 months and the Gross yield on its value: that rent as a percentage of its value now. With less than a year of rent recorded, the page tells you the yield is understated.

Add a car financed by a loan

  1. Open Insights, then Holdings, tap Add and choose the Type: Vehicle. Enter the name, the quantity 1 and the car's value.
  2. In What it cost (optional), enter the full price.
  3. Turn on Financed by a loan.
  4. Enter the Amount financed. Once both figures are typed, Damla shows the Down payment — the price minus the loan.
  5. Choose the account the down payment comes from under Paid from.
  6. Enter the instalment, how often it repeats, the number of instalments, the date of the first one, and the account that pays them (Instalments paid from).

When you save, the down payment leaves your account, a loan is created for the rest, and its instalments go into your forecast and stop after the last one. The car and the loan count once each in your net worth: the car's value and the loan's debt.

Reading the value

  • A holding with no value recorded yet is shown At cost until you enter one.
  • Every value is dated, and each holding keeps its history: the page has a Value over time chart and a Values on file list, where the newest value is the one in force.
  • A vehicle loses value with age, so Damla does not show a loss against what you paid. Its page shows how its value changed since your last update, worded as a change and shown in grey, and a vehicle is left out of the gain-on-investments figure.
  • Gains and changes are always shown with a sign and an arrow, not by colour alone.

Tip: You set the rhythm for updating a value: type a new one whenever you have a figure you trust. The worth shown is always the newest value on file, and the change shown is measured against the value recorded just before it.

Good to know

  • An off-plan property you are paying in stages is set up differently, as a staged purchase. When it is handed over, the purchase's page has Handed over: move to Investments: you enter the market value, it becomes a holding worth that value, and what you paid stays as its cost. See Staged purchases.
  • The instalments of a loan taken out on a financed holding are part of your forecast. To see how your balance moves over the weeks ahead, see Forecast and safe to spend.
  • Property sits in the same list and the same totals as your other holdings.
