Screenshots of your bank app

Update from a screenshot of your bank app's transaction list.

On this page
  • When to use it
  • Step by step
  • Good to know
  • Related

Damla can update an account from a screenshot of your bank app's account page or card page. Your phone reads the text; the pictures are not kept. Nothing is written until you check it and tap.

When to use it

  • You want to set or correct a balance quickly, without filling in forms.
  • You are adding a card and want everything its bank page shows: what you owe, the last statement and its due date.
  • You want to bring several accounts up to date in one go. You can pick up to ten screenshots at a time.

Step by step

  1. In your bank app, open the page that shows the amounts: the account page with the balance, or the card page with the amount owed, the last statement amount, its minimum and its due date. Take a screenshot.
  2. In Damla, tap the plus button, then Update from screenshots.
  3. Tap Choose screenshots and pick one or several pictures.
  4. Wait while Damla reads each picture on the phone. You see Reading…, then one section per account.
  5. Check each section: which account the picture matches, or a new one with a suggested name and a bank Damla recognised. You can edit a new account's name before saving.
  6. For a card, say the day the statement closes if Damla asks, and say which account pays the card.
  7. Tap Add it for a new account, or Update to match for one you already have. When the books already agree with the picture, the section says Already matches your books.

Good to know

Tip: Take screenshots of the page that shows the amounts. A page with only a list of transactions has no balance to read.

What it reads. Damla looks for labelled values, such as "Available balance", "Outstanding", "Statement balance", "Minimum due" and "Due date", and takes the value next to the label. The longest matching label wins, so "minimum due" is never read as "due". The labels, and the on-screen text reading, work in English and Turkish for now. The currency used is the currency code printed most often on the page. When no currency code is printed in capitals, Damla uses your main currency.

Card pages. On a card page, a plain "balance" is what you owe. Damla splits it into the last statement still to pay and what you spent since, and keeps the statement's due date if the page shows one. If the page shows only the limit and the available credit, Damla works the owed amount out as the limit minus the available credit, and says so.

Naming a new account. A new account is named after the page heading (for example "NeoPlus"), with the bank name in front when the picture names one, and it gets that bank's logo. Damla also keeps the last digits of an account or card number it can see on the page, so later bank alerts find the account on their own.

When the books differ. Each section shows the bank's figure beside Damla's: Bank shows and Damla had for an account, Owed now and Damla had for a card. Saving adds an adjustment that brings the account to what the bank shows: one for an account's balance, and for a card up to two, one for the last statement and one for what you spent since. An adjustment is not income and not spending.

Warnings. If something in the picture looks inconsistent — the total owed lower than the statement amount, or the minimum due higher than the statement amount — a warning line appears on the section, above the button.
