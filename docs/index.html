Know every day whether you will have enough money until your next income, and what happens if you spend, commit or delay something.

Coming to the App Store

For iPhone. Requires iOS 27.

What it is

Damla is a cash-flow app for a household. You tell it what happens, in plain sentences or by importing a statement, and it keeps the books correctly and shows what is safe to spend. It is built to be calm: one clear answer a day, with the working behind it.

Today

  • How much is safe to spend, and until when
  • What is coming up: income, bills, instalments
  • A forecast of your balance ahead, and a what-if for a purchase

The books

  • Accounts, cards, loans, people and debts
  • Instalment plans and staged purchases
  • Bank statements imported from PDF or CSV, read on your phone

The maths is done by ordinary, tested code: balances, instalments and forecasts. When you type a sentence such as a school payment on an instalment plan, the app's on-device language model only helps read it, and anything that creates an obligation is shown for your confirmation first.

Private, and on your phone

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.
  • Your data lives on your phone, protected by Face ID or your passcode.
  • It works offline. A few small lookups are optional and described in the privacy policy.
  • You choose where backups go: an encrypted file saved to a folder you pick.

Read the full privacy policy.

What it is not

  • Not financial advice. Damla shows forecasts and estimates from the numbers you give it. They can be wrong. Check important decisions yourself or with a professional.
  • Not a bank and not connected to your bank. It cannot move money.
  • Not on Android, and not on the web. iPhone only.
