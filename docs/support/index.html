Contact

Write to support@raveniq.ai (open in your mail app). Damla is made by RavenIQ.

Damla has no accounts, so there is nothing to look up by username. Please do not send account numbers, statements or screenshots of your finances unless you want to.

Where is my data?

On your phone, in the app's private storage, with iOS Data Protection switched on. Damla has no servers of its own, so we never receive your accounts, balances or transactions.

Face ID and passcode

Damla asks for Face ID or your passcode when you open it, and again after it has been away for the time you choose in Settings. Your face data never leaves iOS; the app only learns that the check passed.

Backup and restore

Damla can save an encrypted backup file to a folder you choose in the Files app, which can be on your phone, in iCloud Drive or on another device. The passphrase is kept in your phone's Keychain. It backs up automatically at most once a day and keeps the last seven. Settings shows when the last backup happened, and the Today screen tells you if it is overdue.

Restore. In Settings, open the backup section, choose the file and enter the passphrase. If you lose the passphrase, the file cannot be opened, so keep a note of it somewhere safe.

Export or delete my data

You can export your transactions as a spreadsheet (CSV) from Settings. To delete everything, delete the app: its data is removed from your phone. Backup files you saved are separate copies, so delete them in the Files app if you no longer want them. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing on our side to delete.

What does it look up online?

Almost nothing, and only the following. Damla works fully offline.

  • Exchange rates, at most once a day. Only currency codes are sent.
  • Prices for holdings you choose to track. This is off by default. Only the ticker symbols are sent, at most once a day.
  • A bank logo, only when you tap “Find logo online”. The bank name you searched for is sent to Wikimedia Commons.

Like any web request, these services can see your IP address. Nothing about your own accounts or amounts is sent.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Typing a sentence such as a payment on an instalment plan uses the language model built into your iPhone when your device and settings support it, and it runs entirely on the phone. Without it, Damla falls back to a simpler reader and to forms, so everything still works.

Importing a bank statement

Choose a PDF or CSV statement from Files. Damla reads it on your phone, never uploads it, and shows what it found for you to check before anything is added. It checks that the amounts add up to the statement balance and flags anything that does not.

Bank texts, emails and Wallet payments

iOS gives apps no access to your messages or mail, so Damla never reads them. They reach it only when you set up a Shortcut (an automation that runs when a message or a Wallet payment arrives) or share one to Damla yourself. Everything is read on your phone and nothing is uploaded.

What to expect. Damla adds a clear bank alert as a transaction, and keeps anything unclear for one tap from you. It ignores adverts, limit and rate notices, reminders and one-time codes, and a bill notice is remembered as a bill, not counted as spending. This works by reading the wording of the message, so it is not perfect:

  • A message from your bank that is really an offer can occasionally be read as a payment, and an unusual wording can be left in the inbox instead of added. Look at Settings › Recent alerts to see what Damla did with each one, and swipe an alert to undo it.
  • Your Shortcut sends every message you point it at. Limit it to your bank’s sender or subject, so unrelated texts and mail are not sent to Damla at all. Texts that do not look like money are dropped without being kept.
  • Nothing creates a commitment, such as a new bill or loan, without your confirmation.
How to report a problem

Email support@raveniq.ai and include:

  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • the Damla version and your iOS version
  • your iPhone model
  • a screenshot, only if it does not show anything private
Is this financial advice?

No. Damla gives information and estimates from the numbers you enter. It is not financial, tax, legal or investment advice, and it does not know your full situation. For important decisions, talk to a qualified professional.
