# Set aside

Set aside is one screen in the Plan tab. It totals up the money a steady month should hold back for costs that do not come every month: bills that arrive now and then, and goals you are saving towards by a date.

## When to use it

- A bill comes every 3 months, 6 months or once a year, and you want to spread it over the months.
- You are saving towards something with a date: a holiday, a deposit, a new car.
- You want the safe-to-spend line on Today to show how much is already set aside for goals.
- You want one number to put aside a month.

## Step by step

1. Make sure the bills themselves are known: open the Plan tab, tap **Income & bills**, and add each bill that repeats every 3 months or less often, with a fixed amount (Every 3 months, Every 6 months, Every year, or Custom… for any rhythm).
2. Open the Plan tab and tap **Set aside**.
3. The card at the top, **Put aside each month**, shows the total to hold back monthly, with the line "So the bills and goals below are covered when they arrive."
4. Under **Bills that come now and then**, each bill from Income & bills is listed with its amount and rhythm on the left and its "a month" figure on the right, plus the next date it is due.
5. Under **Goals**, tap **Add** (the plus button) to save towards something.
6. In the **New goal** form, give the goal a **Name**, a **Target** amount, and the date it is **By**.
7. Below, under **Saved**, put anything you have already saved in **Saved so far**.
8. Tap **Save**. The goal's row shows how much is in and the target, with a progress bar, and either "Reached" or its "a month" figure until the date you chose.
9. To add money later, tap the goal's row and use **Put in more**. A goal you no longer want has **Delete this goal** in its own sheet.

## How the monthly figure is worked out

Damla divides what is still to save by the calendar months left until the due date, and rounds up to the next whole unit. Part of a month still counts as a month, and there is always at least one. For bills that come now and then, the whole amount is still to come and the date is the next due date. The figure is recalculated from today's date each time, so it changes as the next due date moves.

The total at the top is every bill's figure plus every goal's figure.

> **Limit:** These are your own figures, not advice. Damla shows what a steady month should put aside; whether a target or a date is right is your decision.

## Good to know

- Damla does not move money. The goal form says: "Damla does not move money: this is what you have set aside for it, wherever you keep it." Record real transfers as ordinary moves between accounts, and tap **Put in more** to keep the goal's progress up to date.
- On Today, money you have marked as saved towards a goal is shown as spoken for on the safe-to-spend line: "X of it is set aside for goals: Y free to spend". Safe to spend itself does not change: the money is inside your accounts, and the line only separates it out.
- Only goals and bills in your main currency are listed on this screen.
- A bill appears here only if it is an outflow with a fixed amount and a rhythm of 3 months or longer. A bill marked Amount varies, or a monthly one, stays in Income & bills and the forecast instead of being set aside.
- Change a bill in Income & bills and this screen follows it. Set aside stores no bills of its own, so there is nothing to edit twice.
