# Investments and holdings

The Holdings screen lists what you own and have put a value on: shares, funds, gold and other metals, property, vehicles, and anything else you add. It answers two questions in one place: what your holdings are worth now, and what your gain has been.

![Holdings: what you own and what it is worth.](damla-holdings.png)

## When to use it

- You own shares, funds, gold or another asset and want its value counted in your finances.
- You buy more of something, or sell some, and want the cost, the proceeds and the gain worked out.

Holdings live in Insights, under **Holdings**. The same items also appear under Accounts, Investments.

## Step by step

### Add a holding

1. Open **Insights**, then **Holdings**, then tap the **Add** button (the plus sign).
2. Choose the **Type**: Stock or fund, Property, Vehicle, Gold or other metal, or Other. The type decides what the holding's page offers, such as a ticker field.
3. Enter the name, the currency, the units, and the value of one unit today.
4. For a stock or fund, enter its ticker (for example `WVRA.L`) if you want its price looked up online.
5. In **What it cost (optional)**, enter what you paid in total. Leave it empty, or enter 0, for shares you received free, such as vested RSUs. They cost nothing, so their whole value counts as gain.
6. Under **Paid from**, choose an account if you are buying it now, or **Already owned** if no account balance should change.

### Keep the value current

1. Open the holding's page. Under **Update the value**, enter the quantity and the value of one unit, then tap **Save**.
2. To correct an old figure, go to **Values on file** and tap the value. Swipe one to delete a mistake.
3. On the holding's page, tap **What it cost** to correct what you paid. This fixes the record; no account balance changes.
4. Tap **More** to rename it, change its type, or use **Buy more…** and **Sell…**.

The newest value on file is the one in force, and a value you type beats a fetched one from the same day. Until a holding has a value on file, its row reads **At cost, no price yet** and is valued at what you paid.

### Buy more

1. On the holding's page tap **More**, then **Buy more…**.
2. Enter the units added, the price per unit today, and what you paid in total. The total fills in as units times price until you type your own; enter 0 for shares that vest or are granted free.
3. Under **Paid from**, choose the account it was paid from, or **No account (already paid, or free)**.
4. Check the **After this** preview — units, total cost, average cost per unit — then tap **Save**.

### Sell

1. On the holding's page tap **More**, then **Sell…**.
2. Enter the units to sell (all of them is the default), the price per unit, any fees, and the date.
3. Choose **Money goes to**: the account that receives the money. If it is in another currency, also enter the amount that actually arrived, so Damla keeps both sides and the rate it implies.
4. Check the **What this does** preview — proceeds, fees, what you receive, the cost of these units and the gain or loss — then tap **Sell**.

The account receives the proceeds minus fees, and the fees are booked as a cost. The units sold take their share of the cost at the average cost per unit; the difference is booked as income in **Investment gains**, or as a cost in **Investment losses**. Selling everything removes the holding from your investments and your net worth; its history stays.

## Reading the report

The hero card at the top of Holdings shows what your holdings are worth (**Worth now**) and your **Gain on investments**, with a percentage when there is a cost to measure the gain against. Below it:

- **Where it is**: a donut chart of how the holdings are split, each slice labelled by name, share and value.
- Each holding's row shows its value, and a small trend line once there are at least two values on file.
- Gains and losses are shown with a sign and an arrow, and a percentage where one can be worked out — never by colour alone.

> **Limit:** A vehicle is left out of the gain on investments: it loses value with age, so its page shows how its value changed since you last updated it, in grey, as a change rather than a loss. Damla does not calculate tax, and it uses average cost rather than tracking purchases as separate lots.

> **Note:** A holding in a currency Damla has no exchange rate for is left out of the totals, and the screen says so. A pill at the top warns you when the oldest price on file is more than a week old.

## Good to know

- Price lookup is optional and off by default: with a ticker saved and the switch on, prices refresh about once a day, and only the ticker symbols are sent anywhere. See [Prices and exchange rates](/guide/holdings/prices-and-exchange-rates/).
- A property with rent linked to it shows the rent received over the last 12 months and its gross yield. See [Property and cars](/guide/holdings/property-and-cars/).
- Your holdings count towards your net worth at the value you gave them, not only at cost. See [Trends and net worth](/guide/insights/trends-and-net-worth/).
