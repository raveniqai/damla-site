# The free trial and the subscription

Damla is free for 45 days, then one subscription - Damla Core, monthly or yearly - keeps everything open. There are no feature tiers: the subscription unlocks editing, or the app is read-only.

You can start and use the trial without entering any payment details.

## When to use it

- When you first wonder what Damla costs and what happens when the trial ends.
- When you saw a card about the trial on Today and want to know what it means.
- If the trial has ended and you want to know what you can still do.
- When the subscription is running and you want to check, restore or manage it.

## What the trial is

- The 45 days start when you set up your first book in Damla, not when you install the app.
- The trial is tracked on the phone. You are never asked for payment details during it, because it is not the App Store's introductory offer.
- It does not restart if you reinstall the app or delete your data. A copy of the trial record survives both, so the clock keeps running from where it is.
- During the trial, everything works: adding, changing, forecasts, What if, insights.
- In the last three days of the trial, Today shows a one-line banner with the days left and a **Subscribe** button.

## How to subscribe

1. Open Settings, then **Subscription**, and tap the row to open the Damla Core screen (the same screen the Subscribe buttons on Today open). Your current state appears first: how many free days are left, or which plan you have and when it renews.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**. The price shown is the localized price from the App Store. If prices have not loaded, nothing is shown instead.
3. Tap **Subscribe**. Payment is charged to your Apple ID, and the subscription renews automatically until you cancel it in Settings on your iPhone under your name, Subscriptions.
4. If you subscribe while the trial is still running, your plan starts today. The remaining free days are not added to it.
5. Lower on the same screen are two more buttons. **Restore purchases** brings back a subscription you already bought on this Apple ID if it did not arrive. **Manage subscription** opens Apple's own subscription page and appears only when you have a subscription.

## After the trial ends

1. Today shows a card saying the trial has ended and that Damla is read-only for now, with a **Subscribe** button to open the plans.
2. In read-only you can still open every screen: read your transactions, accounts and charts, use the forecast, export your data to a spreadsheet, back it up, restore a backup, and delete your data. Settings, this guide and Restore purchases all stay open.
3. What stops is adding and changing: new transactions, new accounts, new plans for the future, and edits.
4. When an edit stops, an explanation appears once at the bottom of the screen - never as an error box - with two choices, **Subscribe** and **Not now** (when the reason is a failed renewal, only **Not now**).
5. Nothing deletes your data when the trial or subscription ends. Subscribing again unlocks the same books exactly where you left them.

> **Note:** Check the price on the Damla Core screen at the moment of purchase before you tap Subscribe.

## Good to know

> **Note:** Once, during the trial, a card may appear on Today after Damla has done something for you - it says what (for example how many transactions were added from your bank messages by themselves, or a bill matched to the charge that paid it). It does not move the end of the trial and can be dismissed with **Not now**.

> **Limit:** The trial clock is measured in days and never resets, so an App Store reinstall does not earn new days. The remaining time comes from two copies of the record: one in the Keychain, and one in Damla's own store that travels with a backup.

> **Note:** When a renewal fails, Apple's billing grace period keeps Damla working while Apple retries the payment, and the app tells you to update your payment method. If the retries run out, Damla goes read-only until the payment succeeds; then it picks up where it left off.
