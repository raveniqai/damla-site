# Paste or share a message

When a bank message did not reach Damla by itself, you can hand it over: copy the text and paste it into Add by sentence, or share it from Messages with the share sheet. Sharing behaves like the automatic alert: clear payments are added at once, anything unclear waits in the Inbox. Pasting shows you what Damla found first, and you confirm before anything is saved.

## When to use it

- A bank text or email arrived and no automation caught it.
- You have not set up the Shortcut for that bank yet.
- You want to see what Damla makes of a message before anything is saved.

## Set up the share sheet (once)

1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap the plus button and add the action **Add to Damla**.
2. In its Text field, tap the Shortcut Input suggestion above the keyboard so it appears as a blue pill. If you type the words "Shortcut Input" yourself, they are sent as plain text and nothing works.
3. Tap the info button and turn on **Show in Share Sheet**, accepting Text.
4. Name it **Damla**.

After that, when you share text, Damla appears under Shortcuts.

## Paste a message

1. Copy the message text.
2. Tap the plus button on Today or Activity, then **Type a sentence**.
3. Tap **Paste**. The text goes into the field and is read straight away.
4. Read what Damla found under Check and save, then tap **Save**. If something is missing, tap **Complete it** and finish in the form.

## Share a message

1. In Messages, select the bank message text and tap Share.
2. Tap **Damla** under Shortcuts.

The Shortcut runs the same reading as the automation: a clear payment is added at once, and anything unclear waits in the Inbox for one tap.

## What Damla does with the message

When you paste, the result is shown on the app screen, and nothing is saved until you confirm:

- A clear purchase, refund or income, where the card or account digits match one of your accounts: it is shown under Check and save for one tap.
- Something that would create a commitment, such as putting a purchase on instalments: the plan is shown and needs **Create plan**.
- A card statement notice: Damla asks you to **Review the statement**.
- Something unclear: the screen says what is missing and offers **Complete it** to finish it in the form.
- Something that looks already recorded: the screen says "This looks like something already recorded." and offers **Save anyway**. Check it first - paying twice is possible.

On both paths:

- A bill notice ("your bill is ready"): not a transaction - it is remembered as an expected bill, and the charge that later pays it is the one real transaction.
- When you share through the Shortcut, an unclear message - or one that commits future money - waits in the Inbox for one tap (see The inbox).

> **Note:** If the message is a bank alert in the usual shape, code reads it. Anything else goes through the sentence reader: where the iPhone supports Apple Intelligence, the on-device model reads the words and nothing is sent anywhere, and every amount, name and date it returns must appear in your text or it is ignored. Where it is not available, Damla uses a simpler reader and asks you to check the result before saving.

After you save a bank message that printed the balance afterwards, Damla compares it with its books. If they disagree, the screen says "Saved. One more thing" and offers **Match the bank's balance** - a correction that is not income or spending - or **Leave it**.

## Good to know

> **Tip:** Paste and share handle one message at a time. To catch messages as they arrive, set up the Shortcut (see Bank texts and emails).
