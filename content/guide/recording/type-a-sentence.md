# Type a sentence

Type what happened - "Lunch 45 at Zuma from Visa" - and Damla reads it into an amount, an account, a category and a date. You check what it found and confirm with one tap. Nothing is saved until you do.

It is a form that fills itself in from your own words. If Damla reads something wrong or misses a part, you finish the rest in the normal form, starting from what it understood.

![Type a sentence and Damla fills the form.](damla-add.png)

## When to use it

- When you just paid for something or received money, and you want to record it without filling a form.
- When you got a bank message or an email about money that Damla did not catch automatically: paste it in.
- When you want to turn a card purchase into instalments by saying so, for example "school payment on the Everyday card, now 6 months".
- When you want to note a bill: a message that a bill is ready or due, with its amount or a due date, is remembered as a bill coming.

## Step by step

1. Tap the plus button on Today or Activity.
2. Tap **Type a sentence**. The Add by sentence screen opens with the keyboard ready.
3. Write what happened in your own words, with the amount, an account or card name, and a date if it was not today. The example shown in the field is "For example: lunch 45 at Zuma from Visa". You can also write a commitment, such as putting a purchase on instalments.
4. Tap **Continue**. The button shows Reading… while Damla reads the words, then the section called Check and save shows what it found.
5. If everything is right, tap **Save**. If a part is missing or unclear, Damla says what it needs and offers **Complete it**, which opens the form with what was understood already filled in. Finish it there and save.

Two shortcuts:

- **Paste** puts the clipboard in the field and reads it straight away, without another tap. This is for bank texts or emails you copied.
- A message that arrives from your Shortcut, the share sheet or the inbox opens this same screen already read: the first thing you see is the Check and save section.

## What you can see in Check and save

- An ordinary payment or income: the amount, the accounts it moves between, the payee and the date. Tap Save.
- Something that looks already recorded: the section warns "This looks like something already recorded." and offers Save anyway. Check it first - paying twice is possible.
- A purchase turned into instalments: the reading shows the instalment, the number of months, the first date and any fee, with the note "This creates a commitment, so please check it." The button is **Create plan**. It stays a suggestion until you tap it.
- Something incomplete: "I need a bit more:" lists the questions, such as "How much?" or "Which account does it come from?", and **Complete it** opens the form.
- A bill notice: "This is a bill notice." Damla remembers it as an expected bill. The card charge that later pays it is the one real transaction, so nothing is counted twice.
- A card statement notice: "This is a card statement notice." Tap **Review the statement** to go through it.

If the sentence is a bank alert with the amounts printed in the usual shape, code reads it. Only sentences that do not look like an alert go through the sentence reader.

## Good to know

> **Note:** Where the iPhone supports Apple Intelligence, the on-device model reads your words - nothing is sent anywhere. Every amount, name and date the model returns must appear in your sentence or it is ignored, so a number it got wrong cannot creep in silently. The account and category names it returns must match your real accounts, or your own words for them are kept instead.

> **Limit:** If Apple Intelligence is not available on your phone, Damla uses a simpler on-device reader. The screen says so and asks you to check the result before saving; missing parts open the form with **Complete it**.

> **Limit:** Damla reads better when you use the names you actually use for accounts. If it cannot tell which account you meant, it asks, rather than guessing.

> **Note:** No transaction or plan is written to your books until you tap Save, Save anyway or Create plan. Anything that creates a commitment, or that Damla is unsure about, is always shown for one tap first.

After you save, Damla remembers how you categorised the payee, so the next message from the same shop is filed in that category on its own.
